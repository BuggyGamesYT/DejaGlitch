# Déjà Glitch

You're stuck in a looping simulation, and glitches are the only way out.

Walk the same street over and over. Spot a glitch (a black cat walking by twice, a repeating stranger, a wall dissolving into green code, a missing texture...)? Turn back. Nothing off? Keep going. Every glitch you use gets patched by the Agents, so you'll have to find new ones. Jump the Agents, and hold slow-mo to time it. Six sectors to escape.

Inspired by The Matrix and The Exit 8. Not affiliated with or endorsed by their makers. All art, characters, and sounds are original.

- Play in your browser: https://buggygamesyt.github.io/dejaglitch/
- Android: grab `DejaGlitch.apk` from the latest release.

Controls: ← → / A D walk, ↑ / W jump, hold Space / Shift for slow-mo, P pause, M mute. On a phone: on-screen buttons.
